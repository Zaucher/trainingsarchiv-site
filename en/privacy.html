Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This is a translation of the German original. Where the two differ, the German version applies.

In short: This policy applies to Spurlog on iPhone and on Android. Spurlog collects no personal data. Everything you import is stored and processed only on your device. There is no account, no server, no tracking, no analytics tools and no advertising.

1. Controller

Dominik Allgeier
Zum Stegacker 21
87541 Bad Hindelang, Germany
Email: trainingsarchiv.app@gmail.com

2. Which data the app processes

The app only processes data that you import yourself: training files (FIT, GPX, TCX), export archives (ZIP, GZ), for example from Garmin, Strava, Suunto or COROS, or workouts from Apple Health or, on Android, from Health Connect. This data can contain times, duration, distance, heart rate, elevation and GPS location tracks of your activities.

This data is stored only locally on your device (iPhone or Android device), in the app’s protected storage area. It is not transmitted to me or to third parties. I have no access to it.

3. Import from Apple Health (iPhone)

You can import workouts from Apple Health, including those recorded by your Apple Watch. The app asks for your permission at the first import. You decide which types of data Spurlog may read, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in the Health app or in your iPhone’s settings.

Only this data is read: workouts with sport type, start, end, elevation gain and the recording source (for example your Apple Watch), plus the GPS route, heart rate, distance, active energy, running or cycling power and laps.

The app only reads when you start the import yourself, not in the background. It writes nothing to Apple Health. Imported workouts are stored only locally in the app, like imported files. They are not transmitted to me or to third parties and are not used for advertising or other purposes.

3a. Import from Health Connect (Android)

On Android you can import workouts from Health Connect, for example those your watch has written there through its app. The app asks for your permission during the import using the Health Connect dialog. You decide which types of data Spurlog may read, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in Health Connect or in your device’s settings. You delete activities that were already imported in the app.

Only this data is read: workouts with sport type, start, end and laps, plus the exercise route, heart rate, distance, power, elevation gained and active calories burned. Workouts older than 30 days are only read if you additionally allow that in the Health Connect dialog.

The app only reads when you start the import yourself, not in the background. It writes nothing to Health Connect. Imported workouts are stored only locally in the app. They are not transmitted to me or to third parties, not sold and not used for advertising or other purposes. The use of data from Health Connect complies with Google’s Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3b. Permissions

Beyond that, the app asks for no permissions: not for your location, camera, photos or notifications. Location data only exists if it is contained in imported files or Health workouts. On Android the app also uses internet access, but only for the map (section 6) and for purchases through Google Play (section 8).

4. No account, no server, no tracking

The app needs no user account and I run no server of my own. No analytics, tracking or advertising services are used and no device identifiers are collected. The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

5. Export and sharing

When you export data or send it to another app with the share sheet, this happens only through your explicit action. The app you choose, or its provider, is then responsible for further processing.

6. Map view and summit names

iPhone: To display your routes the app uses Apple Maps (MapKit). Your device loads map sections directly from Apple. For technical reasons Apple may process data such as your IP address and the map section shown; Apple’s privacy policy applies. Your training data itself is not transmitted.

Android: For the map background the app loads map tiles, the map style and fonts from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), a free map service based on OpenStreetMap data, and terrain data for the relief from the Terrain Tiles dataset provided by Amazon Web Services (registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles). For technical reasons these services receive your IP address, the tiles of the map section shown and the device type in the user agent of the map library. The app sends them no route, no location and no training data. Tiles are only loaded when you open an activity with a route and an internet connection exists; without a connection the app shows the route without a map background. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a map display without an account (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). With Amazon Web Services, data may be transferred to the USA; Amazon is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

The app assigns summit names with a built-in list directly on the device. No data is transmitted for this.

7. Shortcuts and Siri (iPhone)

The app offers shortcuts. If you use them through Siri, Apple processes the voice request under Apple’s privacy policy. The app itself transmits no data to me in the process.

8. Purchases on the App Store and Google Play

The full version is offered as an in-app purchase on the App Store. Payment is handled exclusively by Apple. I receive no payment data and no information that identifies you personally. The app only checks locally on the device whether the purchase exists. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

On Android the full version is offered through Google Play. Payment is handled exclusively by Google. Here too I receive no payment data, and the app checks through Google Play whether the purchase exists. The Google purchase library included for this can transmit technical data about the purchase to Google; Google is responsible for that. Google’s privacy policy applies.

9. Backups

If you use iCloud backups or backups through your computer, the app’s data can be part of that backup. You control this yourself in your device’s settings; Apple’s terms apply. On Android the app does not take part in Android’s automatic backup to Google Drive.

You can also set up an automatic backup in the app to a folder of your choice, for example in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or on a USB stick. The app then places a copy of your archive in exactly that folder and nowhere else. If the folder is with a cloud service such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, your device transfers the copy to that provider; its terms apply. I receive no data in this case either.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete individual activities in the app. When you delete the app, all data it stored is removed from the device. Copies that you placed in a folder of your own by backup stay there until you delete them yourself.

11. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete the message once it has been dealt with and no statutory retention periods prevent it.

My mailbox is provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the adequacy decision of the EU Commission (Art. 45 GDPR).

12. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time and the page requested, in order to deliver the website and protect it against abuse. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure and reachable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the adequacy decision of the EU Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). How long GitHub stores this data is set out in GitHub’s privacy statement.

The website sets no cookies, uses no JavaScript and loads no content such as fonts or images from third-party servers.

13. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR). Because the app stores no data about you with me, in practice this mainly concerns email enquiries.

Right to object: Where I process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal email is enough.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

14. Changes

If the app changes in a way that means other data is processed, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
