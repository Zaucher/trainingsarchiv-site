Support

Questions, bugs or wishes about Spurlog

Write to me at trainingsarchiv.app@gmail.com. It helps to include your iOS version, the app version (Settings in the app) and a short description. For import problems, please say which device or service the file comes from. Only send training files if you are comfortable with that, because they can contain location data.

Common questions

Which files can I import?

FIT, GPX and TCX files (also compressed as .gz) and the full data export from Strava (ZIP). You can send files to Spurlog from the Files app or the share sheet. From version 1.1, Spurlog also imports workouts from Apple Health, including those from an Apple Watch.

How do I get my Strava export?

On strava.com, go to Settings → My Account → “Download or Delete Your Account” and request an export. Strava sends you a link by email. You can then import the ZIP file straight into Spurlog.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All data stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Only if you set up a backup to a cloud folder such as iCloud Drive yourself does a copy end up there. Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens if I import the same file twice?

Nothing bad. Activities that were already imported are recognized and not created twice.

How do I get my data back out?

With the export in the app: single activities as FIT, GPX or CSV, or the whole archive as a ZIP. Your raw data, the activity list, the detail view and the export are always free.

What does the full version include?

The full version is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It unlocks analyses such as training load, personal bests and the year in review.

Does the purchase cover my family?

Yes, the full version supports Apple’s Family Sharing.

I have a new iPhone. How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, tap “Restore Purchases” in the unlock view and sign in with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
